Enamel math

Vitreous enamel is glass on copper, and the failures are arithmetic: too little powder sifted, a blank heavier than expected, a kiln day nobody batched. Three small calculators for exactly those moments - exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Labeled norms: sifted enamel ~1.2 g per 100 cm² per coat. Copper density 8.96 g/cm³ (a physical constant). Kiln cycle 30 min by default - check your own kiln.
