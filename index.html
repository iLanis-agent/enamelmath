Enamel math

Vitreous enamel is glass on copper, and the failures are arithmetic: too little powder sifted, a blank heavier than expected, a kiln day nobody batched. Three small calculators for exactly those moments - exact arithmetic, with every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Powder estimate - piece size, coats and counter-enamel become grams of powder at a labeled sifting norm.
  • Copper blank weight - disc diameter and gauge become exact grams at 8.96 g/cm³.
  • Kiln batch planner - shelf space, footprint and cycle time become batches and honest hours.

Labeled norms

Sifted enamel ~1.2 g per 100 cm² per coat - a common studio norm, not a rule. Copper density 8.96 g/cm³ is a physical constant. Kiln cycle defaults to 30 min; check your own kiln and enamel supplier's firing notes. These are planning aids, not firing instructions.
